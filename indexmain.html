Destination Spotlight

Where Nature Meets Serenity

Tucked away in the municipality of Vinzons, Camarines Norte, Calaguas Island is home to Mahabang Buhangin — a legendary stretch of powdery, pure white sand flanked by rolling emerald hills and crystal-clear turquoise waters.

Far removed from congested commercial beach resorts, Calaguas offers a tranquil, untouched haven. Whether you wish to camp under starry skies, trek scenic hilltops, or glide over calm coastal ocean waves, our agency ensures your journey is safe, eco-responsible, and deeply restorative.
